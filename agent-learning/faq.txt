Frequently Asked Questions

Order Tracking
Once your order ships, you receive a tracking link by email within 24 hours. Standard
orders update once per day; express orders update in near real time. If tracking has not
updated in 5 days, contact support with your order number. Lost-in-transit orders are
investigated with the carrier and, if confirmed lost, are replaced or refunded at no
extra cost after a 10 business day carrier investigation window.

Payment Methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. Gift cards can be combined
with one other payment method but not with store credit in the same transaction. Failed
payments are retried automatically twice, 30 minutes apart, before the order is
cancelled. We do not accept cash on delivery in any region currently.

Account and Login
Accounts can be created with email or through Google/Apple sign-in. Password resets are
sent to the account email and expire after 1 hour. If you no longer have access to your
account email, support can verify identity using your last 3 order numbers before
manually resetting access. We do not store raw passwords; support cannot read or tell you
your existing password under any circumstance.

International Orders
We currently ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia in addition to domestic
orders. International orders take 10-15 business days and customs/import duties are the
customer's responsibility, charged separately by the carrier on delivery. International
orders cannot use express shipping. Returns from international addresses must be
requested within 21 days of delivery, not the standard 14, due to longer transit time.

Sizing and Fit
Our sizing runs true to standard US sizing for tops and true-to-size with a half-size-up
recommendation for shoes. A full size chart with chest, waist, and inseam measurements in
both inches and centimeters is linked on every product page. If a customer is between two
sizes, we recommend sizing up for outerwear and sizing down for fitted items.

Gift Orders
Gift orders can include a free printed note with a custom message up to 200 characters,
added at checkout. Gift orders never include the price on the packing slip. Gift
recipients can initiate their own return or exchange using the order number, even without
the original purchaser's account, but refunds are issued to the original payment method,
not to the recipient.
