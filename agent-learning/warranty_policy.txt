Warranty Policy

- All electronics carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship.
- Warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or normal wear and tear.
- To file a warranty claim, contact support with your order number and a description of the defect.
- Approved warranty claims are resolved by repair, replacement, or store credit, at our discretion.
- Extended warranty (2 additional years) can be purchased at checkout for eligible electronics.
