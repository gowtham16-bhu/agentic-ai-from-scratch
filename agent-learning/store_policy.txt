Return Policy

- Items can be returned within 14 days of delivery.
- Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original packaging with tags attached.
- Refunds are processed within 5 business days of us receiving the returned item.
- Original shipping cost is refunded only if the item was defective or shipped in error.
- Damaged or defective items can be returned at any time within 60 days of delivery.
- Store credit is issued instantly; refunds to the original payment method take 3-7 business days to appear.
